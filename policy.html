Privacy Policy: Trailog – Travel Diary

Last updated: [date]

Trailog is a travel diary. Your trips, places and notes are stored on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

  • Trip names, dates and notes.
  • The coordinates you save when you tap Check in here.
  • Step counts recorded while a trip screen is open during the trip dates.
  • The photo list shown in the trip gallery. The gallery reads photos on your device only.

Permissions

  • Location is read only when you tap Check in here, and only while Trailog is open. Trailog never tracks your location in the background.
  • Activity recognition is used to count steps with your phone's step counter while a trip screen is open. Steps are saved on this phone.
  • Bluetooth shows whether headphones or car audio are connected. This is shown on screen only and is not saved.
  • Photos are shown only for your trip dates. You can share only some photos if you prefer.

Each permission is requested only when you tap the feature that needs it, after an in-app explanation. You can deny any permission and still use the rest of the app. You can change permissions at any time in Trailog Settings or in Android Settings.

Data sharing

Device and usage data is sent over HTTPS to our servers for [your real one-line purpose].

Touch and screen use

  • Your touches, gestures and screen positions in Trailog
  • The screens you open
  • How fast you type, but not what you type

Motion

  • Motion, accelerometer, gyroscope and step sensor readings

Location

  • Approximate and precise location, and whether a mock location is active

Network

  • Network type, your IP address, and whether a VPN is active

Device

  • Battery, charging, power-saving and temperature state
  • Device details such as screen size, storage, system settings, and whether USB debugging is on
  • Device integrity checks such as boot and OS signature state
  • Whether headphones or other wireless audio are connected
  • The number of images and screenshots on the device, as counts only

Apps

  • The number of apps installed, the install date of this app, and how long you use Trailog

Performance

  • Frame timing and performance measurements

Nothing is sent unless you tap Accept. Declining does not affect any other feature. You can change this later in Settings.

Deleting your data

In Settings, choose Delete all my data. This removes every trip, check-in and step record from your device and resets your data sharing choice. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

Trailog is not directed at children under 13.

We will update this page and the in-app Privacy Policy screen when the data practices change.
