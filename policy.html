Fitna privacy policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Developer: Shantanu Singh

Contact: shantanu0201singh@gmail.com

Fitna is a fitness and phone-training app. Steps, runs and walks, workout notes, photo references, reflex training and balance-hold results are stored in a database on your phone. Fitna has no accounts, no login, and no social or chat features. The Fitna app itself does not make network requests.

What is collected, and only after you agree

If you tap Agree on the consent screen, the Capture SDK included in Fitna sends data to its server at swiping-pattern.dev.lendenclub.com. The SDK sends these categories:

  • Touch: where and how you touch the screen, and timing. Not the content of what you type.
  • Typing: typing timing measures, not the text you write.
  • Motion: readings from the phone's accelerometer, gyroscope and linear-acceleration sensors.
  • Device: model and settings such as screen, battery, storage, sound and security state.
  • Network: connection type and IP address.
  • Location: location fields such as latitude, longitude, accuracy and provider, where the SDK collects them.
  • Performance: frame timing, CPU load and similar measures.
  • Privacy state: signals such as whether developer options or mock location are on.

The full list of 73 fields, plus structural keys, is shown in the app under Settings → Data we collect.

Why

We use this data for keeping the app secure, understanding how it is used, keeping it running well, and phone-security research.

Your choice

Sharing is off by default. Nothing is sent unless you tap Agree. Tapping No thanks keeps sharing off, and Fitna works fully without it. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings.

Sending in the background

After you agree, the SDK may upload data in the background, including when Fitna is closed, using Android's background work scheduler. Fitna's own features (step counting, run and walk tracking, training, and phone checks) run only while their screen is open.

Who receives the data

Shantanu Singh receives the data sent by the SDK. The server at swiping-pattern.dev.lendenclub.com is operated by Shantanu Singh or by a hosting provider on its behalf: Amazon Web Services (AWS).

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until it is sent, until you turn sharing off, or until you delete your data.
  • On our server: [RETENTION PERIOD].

Your data and how to delete it

  • On your phone: Settings → Delete my data removes runs, workout notes, photo references, steps, balance and reflex history, and clears events not yet sent.
  • On our server: email shantanu0201singh@gmail.com with your phone model and the approximate date you agreed. We will locate and delete the data we hold for that phone.
  • Turning sharing off in Settings stops new sending and clears events not yet sent.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: sending data after you agree, and showing your connection type.
  • Location (foreground only): route, distance and pace while the Run/Walk screen is open.
  • Activity recognition: counting your steps.
  • Photos (Android 13 and newer) or storage (Android 12 and older): attaching workout photos and clearing screenshots you select.
  • Biometrics: the optional app lock. Fitna never sees your fingerprint or face data.
  • Wake lock, boot completed, foreground service and fingerprint: used by the Android background work system that the SDK relies on for uploads.

Fitna does not use background location, Bluetooth, the camera, the microphone, contacts or notifications.

Children

Fitna is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, where required, ask for your consent again.
